description: "Every bank you link becomes its own read-only connection. Plaid bills per bank, so use Plaid for the banks SimpleFIN cannot reach."
---

<Info>
  **Before you start**

  - **A free Plaid developer account** at [dashboard.plaid.com](https://dashboard.plaid.com).
    Postern includes no Plaid access of its own.
  - **Money, per linked bank.** On Production, Plaid bills Transactions monthly for every bank you
    keep linked, and Balance per request. Plaid publishes no price list. A new US or Canada team
    lands on the free Trial plan instead: real bank data, up to 10 linked banks, no security
    questionnaire.
  - **Postern up, and the Console open.** The Console answers at
    [http://localhost:8787](http://localhost:8787). Only the machine Postern runs on can reach it.
    If that machine has no screen, set up [remote access](/start/remote-access) first.
  - **SimpleFIN first.** One SimpleFIN subscription covers every bank it reaches. Plaid bills per
    bank, so use Plaid only for the banks SimpleFIN cannot reach —
    [Connect SimpleFIN](/connect/simplefin).
  - **Plaid's own words.** Item and Link are Plaid's words. An Item is one bank connection at Plaid.
    Link is Plaid's connect-your-bank window.
</Info>

## Choose your path

| If… | Then |
|---|---|
| you have not saved a Plaid app here yet | start at [Create your Plaid developer account](#plaid-account) |
| your app is saved and you want another bank | Console → **Sources** → the **Plaid** group → **Add a bank**, then [Sign in to your bank in the Plaid window](#link-the-bank) |
| a bank reads **consent lapsed** | [Reconnect a bank when its consent lapses](#reconnect) |
| you want to try this without a real bank | [Test on Plaid Sandbox instead](#test-on-plaid-sandbox-instead) |

<Steps>

<Step title="Create your Plaid developer account" titleSize="h2" id="plaid-account">

<Warning>
  Trial counts 10 banks for the lifetime of the team. A bank you remove does not give the slot back.
  To try the flow and spend no slot, [test on Plaid Sandbox
  instead](#test-on-plaid-sandbox-instead).
</Warning>

Open [dashboard.plaid.com](https://dashboard.plaid.com) and create an account. Plaid's dashboard
opens when you finish.

Plaid's Trial plan covers US and Canada teams created on or after 2026-04-15. An older team uses
paid Production access instead. Which plan you hold decides what this page costs you.

</Step>

<Step title="Copy your client ID and Production secret" titleSize="h2" id="keys">

<Note>
  Nobody has captured Plaid's dashboard screens, so this page names addresses rather than buttons.
</Note>

In the Plaid dashboard, open
[dashboard.plaid.com/developers/keys](https://dashboard.plaid.com/developers/keys). Postern's own
Plaid screen names a different path, **Team Settings → Keys**. Nobody has checked which path Plaid
shows today, so use the address.

Copy the `client_id`. Then copy the Production secret.

The `client_id` is the same string in every Plaid environment. Each environment has its own secret.
The Console can use only the Production one.

<Note>
  Some banks send you to their own sign-in page. Those banks can refuse an app whose Application
  Display Info profile is empty, even on Trial. The refusal appears inside the bank's page, not in
  Postern. Fill that profile in at Plaid before you link a bank — the app's display name at minimum.
</Note>

</Step>

<Step title="Paste both into the Console" titleSize="h2" id="paste-the-app">

<Warning>
  Postern encrypts the secret and stores it on your own machine as you save. The Console never shows
  it again: the field afterwards reads *already sealed — leave blank to keep*. Plaid's dashboard is
  the only other place you can read it.
</Warning>

Go to Console → **Sources** → **Add a source** → **Plaid**. Paste the `client_id` into **Client
ID**. Paste the Production secret into **Secret**. Press **Save & add a bank**.

Postern creates a Link session at Plaid and opens it in a new tab. The panel switches to **Link your
banks** and checks every few seconds for the result.

</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Sign in to your bank in the Plaid window" titleSize="h2" id="link-the-bank">

<Warning>
  Banks you linked at my.plaid.com do not transfer, and there is nothing to import. Link each one
  again through this window. On a Trial team, each one costs a slot.
</Warning>

The new tab carries the title **Connect with Plaid**. Its first page reads *Postern uses Plaid to
connect your account*.

Plaid asks for a phone number first. **Continue without phone number** — plain text under the
**Continue** button, not a button — skips it.

Choose your bank. Sign in with the bank's own credentials. Finish. Plaid then shows *Success!* over
*Your information has been successfully shared. It's safe to close the browser now.*

You never paste a bank password into Postern. The sign-in happens on Plaid's page, and only a token
for that one bank comes back.
[Nothing outside your home has to reach your machine for this to work.](/reference/security-model)

Postern sends the United States as the only country, so Link offers US banks. On a Trial team, every
real bank you finish here costs one of the 10.

<Frame caption="Continue without phone number is the line under the Continue button, not a button of its own. This capture is from Plaid's Sandbox.">
  <img
    src="/images/plaid-link-phone-pane.png"
    alt="Plaid's hosted Link page: a phone number field, a grey Continue button, and Continue without phone number as plain text beneath it."
  />
</Frame>

Plaid may show a banner that reads *Postern is testing something new*. Plaid builds that line from the
app name Postern sends. It appears for apps without full Production access, and nothing in the
Console turns it off.

A bank can also need time at Plaid before it works with a new app. Minutes to hours is usual.
Charles Schwab has taken up to about 6 weeks.

If no new tab opened, a pop-up blocker stopped it. The panel behind it carries **Re-open Plaid
Link**, which opens the same session.

</Step>

<Step title="Watch the bank land in the Console" titleSize="h2" id="first-bank">

Leave the **Link your banks** panel open. It checks every few seconds and shows *waiting for the
first bank*.

When you finish at Plaid, the bank appears there with its name and its health. The button changes
from **Continue in Sources** to **Done — go to Sources**. Press it.

**Sources** now carries a **Plaid** group with that bank beneath it. A bank on its first sync reads
**Syncing…**. Plaid's first sync commonly returns nothing, and the history arrives over the syncs
that follow. That is normal.

Postern checks every Plaid bank at the same rate: every 6 hours, and never faster than 15 minutes. A
bank's own page shows that rate read-only under **Poll cadence**, with **Change it on the Plaid
group →** beside it.

<Frame caption="This panel is the Console, not Plaid. Re-open Plaid Link reopens the same session.">
  <img
    src="/images/plaid-waiting-for-the-first-bank.png"
    alt="The Console's Link your banks panel: an indicator reading waiting for the first bank, a Re-open Plaid Link button and a Continue in Sources button."
  />
</Frame>

<Note>
  One session links one bank. The panel says *bank(s)* because that is Plaid's wording.
  [A multi-bank session needs a Plaid user token Postern cannot get.](/reference/security-model)

  For the next bank, go to Console → **Sources**, find the **Plaid** group header, and press **Add a
  bank**. **Client ID** is already filled and the secret stays saved, so leave **Secret** blank.
</Note>

<Note>
  If you close Plaid's tab before you finish, the session ends empty. The panel still shows *waiting
  for the first bank*, its button changes to **Done — go to Sources**, and no bank row appears. You
  linked nothing and spent no slot. A new link lasts 3 hours. Start again from **Add a source** →
  **Plaid**.
</Note>

</Step>

<Step title="Reconnect a bank when its consent lapses" titleSize="h2" id="reconnect">

Bank consent expires about once a year, per bank. Capital One, Fidelity and Bank of America all
enforce it. A lapsed bank returns empty pages, which reads like a quiet week. Postern checks each
bank's standing and shows it instead.

In Console → **Sources**, a lapsed bank reads **consent lapsed** under **Cadence**, and its action
reads **Reconnect →** rather than **Manage →**. Click **Reconnect →**.

On the connection page, press **Reconnect at Plaid**. A new tab opens on Plaid's re-approval page,
and the Console says *Opened Plaid's re-approval page in a new tab*. Sign in to your bank there and
approve.

That re-approval link lasts about 30 minutes, not 3 hours. This screen carries no re-open button. If
a pop-up blocker eats the tab, press **Reconnect at Plaid** again.

The re-approval runs against the same bank — no re-paste, no fresh link. The Item keeps its
identity, carries on from where it stopped, and fills in what it missed. The row clears at the next
check, up to 6 hours away. **Sync now**, on the same page, checks at once.

<Note>
  The re-approval flow repairs neither cause of **dead**.

  Plaid no longer holds that bank's Item. Link the bank again from **Add a source** → **Plaid**. On
  a Trial team that costs a slot.

  Or your Plaid team hit its bank cap, or spent its credits. No re-link can work. Free a slot or
  change your Plaid plan first.

  A connection you disconnected has no credential left on your machine, and the Console says so
  rather than offer a reconnect that cannot work.
</Note>

</Step>

</Steps>

## Test on Plaid Sandbox instead

Sandbox gives you fake banks and the credentials `user_good` / `pass_good`. It spends no Trial slot.

The Console has no environment picker. **Save & add a bank** always saves your app as Production —
the screen's own footnote reads *Console defaults to Production*. A Sandbox secret pasted there
reaches Plaid's production API, which refuses it: no Link session, no new tab, and the Console
reports Plaid's refusal.

<Warning>
  The command below writes over any Plaid app already saved here. Every bank that uses that app
  stops until you save an app again.
</Warning>

Save a Sandbox app straight to Postern. Run this on the machine Postern runs on:

```bash
curl -s -X POST http://localhost:8787/api/plaid/app \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"clientId":"YOUR_PLAID_CLIENT_ID","clientSecret":"YOUR_PLAID_SANDBOX_SECRET","env":"sandbox"}'
```

It answers `{"configured":true,"clientId":"YOUR_PLAID_CLIENT_ID","env":"sandbox"}`. Now go to Console
→ **Sources** → **Add a source** → **Plaid**, leave **Secret** blank, and press **Add a bank**. That
button reuses the saved app and leaves its environment alone, so Link opens on Sandbox. Its grey bar
reads *You are currently in Sandbox mode.*

`.env` is the other route, and it stops short of a Link session. Set `PLAID_CLIENT_ID`,
`PLAID_SECRET` and `PLAID_ENV=sandbox` in `.env`, beside `docker-compose.yml`. Run
`docker compose up -d` again and they point Postern at Plaid's Sandbox host. Postern reads those
three only until you save a Plaid app. The Console cannot drive them: its Plaid screen still reads *not configured*, and its
only button saves a Production app.

To go back to Production, press **Clear** on the **Plaid** row at Console → **Settings** →
**Provider apps**, then paste your Production keys. No bank that used the old app refreshes again
until you save one.

## Confirm it works

- Console → **Sources** carries a **Plaid** group, one row per linked bank, each with an account
  count and a **Cadence** cell. The group's own line says how many banks feed it.
- Each bank's **Cadence** reads a time, not a warning.
- Console → **Settings** → **Provider apps**: **Plaid** reads *configured*.
- A bank that also arrives through SimpleFIN carries *also arrives via SimpleFIN — served as one*.
  Postern merges on its own only when both providers publish the last 4 digits. Otherwise it asks
  *Is this the same account, seen twice?* on its own screen. Answer **They’re the same account** or
  **Keep them separate**, and it stays answered.
- Ask an agent with a **finance** grant: *which finance sources can you see, and how fresh are
  they?* It names the Plaid bank and its health. It names the consent expiry date too, once
  Plaid publishes one. You grant **finance** at Console → **Agents & keys** —
  [Connect an agent](/start/connect-an-agent).

## If something went wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No new tab opened, and the Console reports that Plaid refused your keys | You pasted a Sandbox secret; the Console only sends Production. Copy the Production secret from [the Keys page](https://dashboard.plaid.com/developers/keys) and paste it into **Secret** again — [Copy your client ID and Production secret](#keys). |
| Your bank's own sign-in page refuses the app | Your app's Application Display Info profile is empty. Fill it in at the Plaid dashboard, the display name at minimum, then link the bank again — [Sign in to your bank in the Plaid window](#link-the-bank). |
| A bank reads **dead**, and **Reconnect at Plaid** does not bring it back | Plaid no longer holds that Item: link the bank again from **Add a source** → **Plaid**. Or your Plaid team hit its bank cap or spent its credits: free a slot or change your Plaid plan — [Reconnect a bank when its consent lapses](#reconnect). |
| No bank refreshes any more after you pressed **Clear** in **Settings** → **Provider apps** | Postern no longer holds that app. Paste the `client_id` and the Production secret again — [Paste both into the Console](#paste-the-app). |
| The Console's Plaid screen reads *not configured* after you set the `PLAID_` variables | Environment variables never make that screen ready. Use the POST above — [Test on Plaid Sandbox instead](#test-on-plaid-sandbox-instead). |

## What you have now

One connection for each bank you linked. Finance is read-only and carries no actions. No agent can
move money, and Postern never held the bank sign-in.
[Only Home Assistant carries actions today.](/reference/mcp-primitives)

Postern caches accounts, transactions and holdings on your own machine. An agent with a **finance**
grant answers from that cache. Where a bank also arrives through SimpleFIN, agents get
one merged row, and both provider rows stay readable.
[Plaid fixes the 2-year history window when you link the bank, and only a fresh link widens
it.](/reference/mcp-primitives)

What this costs you from here:

- Plaid bills Production per linked bank, monthly, for as long as the bank stays linked.
- A balance an agent reads from the cache costs nothing. An explicit live balance costs money each
  time: it goes to a Plaid endpoint Plaid bills per call.
- Consent expires about once a year, per bank. Postern shows the lapse but cannot renew it —
  [Reconnect a bank when its consent lapses](#reconnect).
- When you disconnect a bank, Postern tells Plaid to cancel that Item. That also stops Plaid's
  charge for it, and Postern leaves your other Plaid banks alone. It is one-way: you cannot
  reconnect a bank you disconnected or erased, only link it from the start, on a fresh slot.
